Chikmagalur 1 Night / 2 Days Tour Package

A crisp weekend road trip from Bangalore exploring Karnataka's highest peak at Mullayanagiri, Baba Budangiri, and serene coffee plantations.

💬 WhatsApp for Package Quote 📞 +91 8050500030

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Bangalore Pickup → Chikmagalur Town & Lake

Early morning pickup in Bangalore (approx. 4.5 hrs drive). Check in to hotel/homestay.

  • Hirekolale Lake: Picturesque lake surrounded by hill ranges.
  • Coffee Estate Walk: Stroll through fragrant coffee plantations.
  • Chikmagalur Town: Local market visit for coffee powders and spices.

Overnight stay in Chikmagalur.

Day 2

Mullayanagiri, Baba Budangiri & Bangalore Drop

Early morning drive to mountain peaks.

  • Mullayanagiri: Stand on Karnataka's highest peak (1,930m) with sweeping cloud views.
  • Seethalayyanagiri: Scenic mountain temple stop en route.
  • Baba Budangiri: Sacred mountain range and Sufi shrine.
  • Jhari / Hebbe Waterfall: Nature viewpoint (depending on accessibility and timing).

Afternoon departure and late evening drop in Bangalore.
