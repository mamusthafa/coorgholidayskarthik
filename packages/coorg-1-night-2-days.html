Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Mysore Pickup & Kushalnagar Sightseeing → Madikeri

Morning pickup from Mysore railway station, hotel, or residence. Drive towards Coorg (approx. 2.5 to 3 hours). Begin sightseeing at Kushalnagar.

  • Golden Temple (Namdroling Monastery, Bylakuppe): Explore the majestic Tibetan Buddhist monastery and golden statues.
  • Kaveri Nisargadhama: Bamboo groves, deer park, and hanging bridge over River Cauvery.
  • Dubare Elephant Camp: Riverside visit on the banks of River Cauvery.
  • Harangi Dam: Quick scenic photo stop (subject to time & access).
  • Raja's Seat: Proceed to Madikeri in the evening for sunset view across misty valleys.

Overnight stay in Madikeri / Kushalnagar hotel or resort.

Day 2

Madikeri Local Sightseeing & Mysore Return

After breakfast at your stay, proceed with central Madikeri sightseeing.

  • Abbey Falls: Beautiful cascading waterfall amidst lush private coffee and spice plantations.
  • Madikeri Fort: 17th-century historical fort and museum.
  • Omkareshwara Temple: Unique temple displaying Gothic and Islamic architectural styles.
  • Gaddige (Raja's Tomb): Royal tombs built in Muhammadan style.
  • Coffee Plantation & Spice Shopping: Stop for authentic Coorg coffee, homemade chocolates, and spices.

Late afternoon departure from Coorg. Arrive in Mysore by evening for drop at station or hotel.
