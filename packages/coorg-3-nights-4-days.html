Coorg 3 Nights / 4 Days Tour Package

Best suited for travellers seeking an unhurried, immersive hill holiday with time for estate relaxation, nature walks, and optional Mandalpatti jeep exploration.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Mysore Pickup → Kushalnagar Circuit → Coorg Check-in

Doorstep pickup from Mysore or Bangalore. Drive to Kushalnagar.

  • Golden Temple (Bylakuppe): Magnificent Tibetan Buddhist monastery, prayer wheels, and Tibetan handicraft shops.
  • Kaveri Nisargadhama: Ecological park with hanging bridge, bamboo groves, and elephant ride options.
  • Dubare Elephant Camp: Riverside location on the banks of River Cauvery.
  • Evening Check-in: Relax in your hotel / resort amidst greenery.

Overnight stay in Coorg.

Day 2

Madikeri Town & Historical Monuments

After breakfast, explore central Madikeri's key attractions.

  • Abbey Falls: Roaring waterfall amidst coffee and cardamom plantations.
  • Madikeri Fort & Palace: Historic fortress with panoramic city views.
  • Omkareshwara Temple: Unique architectural blend of Gothic and Islamic styles.
  • Gaddige (Raja's Tombs): Kodava monuments built in Muhammadan style.
  • Raja's Seat: Sunset viewpoint and seasonal floral garden.

Overnight stay in Coorg.

Day 3

Talacauvery Pilgrimage & Estate Relaxation / Mandalpatti

Morning journey into the Brahmagiri hills.

  • Bhagamandala: Triveni Sangama holy confluence and Sri Bhagandeshwara Temple.
  • Talacauvery: Source of River Cauvery with breathtaking mountain panorama.
  • Optional Mandalpatti Jeep Safari: Off-road jeep ride to high mountain ridge (arranged optionally based on weather).
  • Coffee Estate Walk: Stroll through coffee estates, enjoy birdwatching, and relax.

Overnight stay in Coorg.

Day 4

Leisure Morning, Spice Shopping & Departure

Breakfast at hotel, leisurely checkout, and final spice/coffee shopping in Madikeri market before returning to Mysore or Bangalore.
