Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Mysore Arrival & Heritage Sightseeing

Pickup in Mysore (or Bangalore). Explore Mysore Palace, Chamundi Hills, St. Philomena's Church, and Brindavan Gardens musical fountain.

Overnight stay in Mysore.

Day 2

Mysore to Chikmagalur & Coffee Country

Drive to Chikmagalur via scenic countryside. Check in to your resort/homestay, explore Hirekolale Lake, and stroll through coffee plantations.

Overnight stay in Chikmagalur.

Day 3

Mullayanagiri Peak & Baba Budangiri Hills

Full day mountain circuit: Mullayanagiri peak (1,930m), Seethalayyanagiri, Baba Budangiri caves, and scenic viewpoints.

Overnight stay in Chikmagalur.

Day 4

Plantation Walk, Coffee Shopping & Departure

Morning nature walk, buy authentic freshly roasted coffee beans and spices, followed by return drop to Bangalore or Mysore.
