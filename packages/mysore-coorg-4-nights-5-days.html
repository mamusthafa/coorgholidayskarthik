Mysore & Coorg 4 Nights / 5 Days Tour Package

A relaxed, comprehensive holiday itinerary for families and groups. Covers royal Mysore heritage, Zoo, Karanji Lake, and complete Coorg sightseeing with dedicated private cab transportation.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Mysore Arrival & Heritage Monuments

Pickup in Mysore/Bangalore. Visit Mysore Palace, Chamundi Hills temple, and St. Philomena's Cathedral.

Overnight stay in Mysore.

Day 2

Mysore Zoo, Karanji Lake & Brindavan Gardens

Full day Mysore leisure: Sri Chamarajendra Zoological Gardens, Karanji Lake nature park, and evening musical fountain show at Brindavan Gardens.

Overnight stay in Mysore.

Day 3

Mysore → Kushalnagar → Coorg Check-in

Drive to Coorg via Golden Temple (Bylakuppe), Kaveri Nisargadhama, and Dubare Elephant Camp. Evening sunset at Raja's Seat.

Overnight stay in Coorg.

Day 4

Coorg Waterfalls & Talacauvery Circuit

Visit Abbey Falls, Madikeri Fort, Omkareshwara Temple, Bhagamandala, and holy Talacauvery atop Brahmagiri hills.

Overnight stay in Coorg.

Day 5

Coffee Estate Walk, Shopping & Departure

Morning coffee plantation walk, spice shopping in Madikeri, and transfer back to Mysore or Bangalore for return journey.
