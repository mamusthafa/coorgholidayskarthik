Chikmagalur 2 Nights / 3 Days Tour Package

The classic Chikmagalur coffee country experience. Climb Mullayanagiri, explore the caves of Baba Budangiri, walk through aromatic coffee plantations, and unwind at Hirekolale Lake with dedicated private cab service.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Bangalore Pickup → Chikmagalur Town & Estate Walk

Morning pickup in Bangalore (or Mysore). Travel through scenic highway towards the Western Ghats (approx. 4.5 hrs).

  • Resort / Homestay Check-in: Unwind amidst nature and coffee aroma.
  • Hirekolale Lake: Serene lake nestled between mountain ranges; ideal for sunset photos.
  • Coffee Estate Walk: Guided stroll to learn how Arabica and Robusta beans are grown and harvested.

Overnight stay in Chikmagalur.

Day 2

Mullayanagiri, Seethalayyanagiri & Baba Budangiri Hills

Full day dedicated to the high-altitude peaks and cloud landscapes.

  • Mullayanagiri Peak: Trek to Karnataka's highest mountain peak (1,930m) for panoramic vistas.
  • Seethalayyanagiri: Beautiful ancient Shiva temple situated on the ridge.
  • Baba Budangiri: Sacred mountain shrine revered by diverse traditions.
  • Manikyadhara Falls / Jhari Falls: Scenic mountain waterfall stops.

Overnight stay in Chikmagalur.

Day 3

Coffee Shopping, Sightseeing & Bangalore Return

Enjoy breakfast at stay, checkout, shop for artisanal coffee and homemade chocolates in Chikmagalur town, and embark on return journey to Bangalore (or Mysore) for evening drop.
