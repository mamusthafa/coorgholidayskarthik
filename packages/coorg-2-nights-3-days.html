Coorg 2 Nights / 3 Days Tour Package

The quintessential Coorg vacation itinerary. Experience the full spectrum of waterfalls, historical sites, Tibetan heritage, coffee plantation life, and holy Talacauvery at a comfortable pace.

💬 WhatsApp for Package Quote 📞 +91 8050500030

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Mysore Pickup → Kushalnagar Sightseeing → Madikeri Check-in

Morning pickup from Mysore railway station, hotel, or Bangalore airport/city. Begin the scenic drive into Kodagu district.

  • Bylakuppe Tibetan Settlement: Visit the famous Golden Temple (Namdroling Monastery) and shop for Tibetan crafts.
  • Kaveri Nisargadhama: Walk across the hanging bridge, explore bamboo thickets and deer park.
  • Dubare Elephant Camp: Riverside location along River Cauvery known for elephant interaction.
  • Harangi Reservoir: Scenic water catchment area.
  • Raja's Seat: Sunset view over undulating mist-covered valleys.

Overnight stay in Madikeri hotel/resort.

Day 2

Madikeri Local Sightseeing & Talacauvery Circuit

After breakfast, depart for a full day of sightseeing across Madikeri and the Western Ghats Brahmagiri range.

  • Abbey Falls: Magnificent waterfall surrounded by lush coffee bushes and pepper vines.
  • Madikeri Fort: 17th-century historical fort housing a museum and palace relics.
  • Omkareshwara Temple: Revered Shiva temple blending Gothic and Islamic architectural elements.
  • Bhagamandala: Holy confluence of Rivers Cauvery, Kannike, and mythological Sujyothi.
  • Talacauvery: The sacred birthplace of River Cauvery situated high on the Brahmagiri hill slopes.
  • Local Spice & Coffee Market: Evening walk through Madikeri market for spices, honey, and coffee.

Overnight stay in Coorg.

Day 3

Coffee Estate Walk, Gaddige & Mysore/Bangalore Return

Enjoy a relaxed morning amidst greenery and fresh mountain air.

  • Coffee Plantation Experience: Guided plantation walk to observe coffee beans, pepper, and cardamom.
  • Gaddige (Raja's Tomb): Royal Kodava monument with views of Madikeri town.
  • Shopping & Departure: Final opportunity for spices and authentic chocolates before checkout.

Drive back to Mysore (approx. 3 hrs) or Bangalore (approx. 5.5 hrs) for evening drop at agreed destination.
