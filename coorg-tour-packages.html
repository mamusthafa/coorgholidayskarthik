Must-Visit Attractions

Major Sightseeing Places in Coorg

Key highlights covered in our Coorg tour package itineraries.

🌊 Abbey Falls

A scenic waterfall nestled among private coffee plantations and spice estates near Madikeri town.

🌅 Raja's Seat

Famous seasonal garden and sunset point overlooking misty valleys, once favored by the Kings of Kodagu.

⛩️ Golden Temple (Bylakuppe)

One of India's largest Tibetan settlements featuring impressive 40-foot gilded Buddha statues.

🐘 Dubare Elephant Camp

River Cauvery bank location known for elephant interactions, boating, and scenic nature views.

⛰️ Talacauvery & Bhagamandala

The holy birthplace of the sacred River Cauvery in Brahmagiri hills, along with Triveni Sangama.

☕ Coffee Plantation Walk

Experience guided estate walks to learn about Arabica & Robusta coffee cultivation, pepper, and cardamom.
