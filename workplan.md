Below is a complete website workplan and content structure for **Coorg Holidays**, designed specifically as a **static, mobile-responsive tour package website hosted on GitHub Pages** and optimized primarily for **Google Ads lead generation through calls and WhatsApp enquiries**.

The package structure uses realistic Karnataka tourism circuits. Mysuru works naturally as a pickup point for Coorg, while Bengaluru can serve as the pickup point for Mysuru, Coorg and Chikmagalur combinations. Official Karnataka Tourism also promotes Mysuru–Coorg itineraries and identifies major attractions such as Mysuru Palace, Chamundi Hills, Abbey Falls, Mullayanagiri and Baba Budangiri. ([Karnataka Tourism][1])

One important content rule for **Coorg Holidays**: don't fabricate fixed hotel names, attraction timings, entrance fees, jeep charges or "guaranteed" sightseeing.
