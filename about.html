About Coorg Holidays

Your trusted local partner for private Karnataka tour packages, verified accommodations, and personalized travel assistance.

Who We Are

Coorg Holidays (coorgholidays.in) is a specialized holiday planning and cab service operator dedicated to delivering seamless private travel experiences across Karnataka's most famous destinations — Coorg (Kodagu), Mysore (Mysuru), and Chikmagalur (Chikkamagaluru).

Unlike mass bus-tour aggregators or crowded group tours, we specialize strictly in customized, private holiday packages. Whether you are traveling as a couple looking for tranquil privacy, a family with children and elders wanting a relaxed pace, or a group of friends planning an adventurous Western Ghats getaway, we provide dedicated private vehicles and handpicked accommodations.

Our Core Principles

🚗 Dedicated Private Cabs

Clean, well-maintained Sedan, SUV, and Tempo Traveller vehicles driven by courteous, hill-experienced drivers who understand local routes and timings.

🏨 Transparent Stay Selections

From budget-friendly homestays to luxury nature resorts with breathtaking balcony views, we only partner with verified properties maintaining high cleanliness standards.

💬 Instant WhatsApp Assistance

Direct communication with trip planners before and throughout your journey for route suggestions, restaurant recommendations, and immediate support.
